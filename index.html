PLANET EXPLORER

You are a planetary researcher. Collect plants, rocks and minerals, and scan the ground for buried traces of ancient life. Bring your samples back to the lander to analyze them, earn Research Points and unlock new worlds.

  • WASD / Arrows move
  • E / Space collect (hold to dig)
  • Q scanner pulse
  • C research codex
  • M star map (at lander)
  • Esc close menus

Watch your oxygen! Liquid pools slow you down and drain oxygen faster. If you black out, a rescue drone brings you back, but your carried samples are lost.
