PLANET EXPLORER

You are a planetary researcher. Collect plants, rocks and minerals, and scan the ground for buried traces of ancient life. Bring your samples back to the lander to analyze them, earn Research Points and unlock new worlds.

  • WASD / Arrows move
  • Mouse look (click to capture)
  • Space jump
  • 1–4 / Wheel switch tool
  • E / Left click use tool
  • R / Right click lower ground (Terraform)
  • [ ] / Shift+Wheel brush size
  • Q quick scanner pulse
  • C research codex
  • M star map (at lander)
  • Esc / P pause

Planets and moons are round: walk all the way around them! Each world has its own gravity, and you only find out if the air is breathable once you land. Watch your oxygen! Seas slow you down and drain oxygen faster. If you black out, a rescue drone brings you back, but your carried samples are lost.
